# Racing Pigeon Research Glossary

| Term | English | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Candidate Gene | Candidate Gene | A gene suspected to be associated with a target trait, based on biological function or existing evidence. |
| Association Study | Association Study | A study design that analyses the statistical association between genotype and trait. |
| Heritability | Heritability | The proportion of phenotypic variation explained by genetic factors. |
| Breeding Value | Breeding Value | An estimate of the genetic contribution an individual passes on to its offspring. |
| Selection Signature | Selection Signature | A characteristic pattern of genetic variation left in the genome by natural or artificial selection. |
| Polygenic Trait | Polygenic Trait | A trait influenced by multiple genes together. |
| Prospective Study | Prospective Study | A study design in which subjects and measurements are defined at the start and followed forward over time. |
| Association | Association | Two variables are statistically related, but this does not mean a causal relationship. |
| Correlation | Correlation | A relationship exists between variables, and it may be influenced by a third variable. |
| Causation | Causation | One variable has been shown to cause changes in another variable. |
| Hypothesis | Hypothesis | A scientific conjecture that is still awaiting testing. |
| Single Nucleotide Polymorphism | SNP | A variation of a single base in the genome; an important genetic basis for differences in traits. |
| Exon | Exon | A DNA segment within a gene that codes for protein. |
| Population Genomics | Population Genomics | The discipline that studies genome variation at the population level. |
| DOI | Digital Object Identifier | A unique identifier for digital objects, used to permanently identify and locate scholarly literature. |
| PubMed | PubMed | A biomedical literature database provided by the U.S. National Library of Medicine. |
| Meta-analysis | Meta-analysis | A statistical method that combines the results of several studies in a quantitative pooled analysis. |
| Randomized Controlled Trial | Randomized Controlled Trial | A clinical trial with random allocation and a control group; causal inference from it is comparatively strong. |
| Cohort Study | Cohort Study | An epidemiological research method that follows a defined population over the long term. |
| Confounding Factor | Confounding Factor | An extra variable that affects how results are interpreted and can interfere with causal judgement. |
| Reproducibility | Reproducibility | The degree to which research results can be independently reproduced and verified by other researchers. |
