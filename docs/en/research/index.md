# Racing Pigeon Research Directions

This site organizes racing pigeon research content under eight directions. Each direction page covers the research background, the key findings and the literature sources, and every conclusion is labelled with its evidence boundary.

## Research directions

- [Genetics research](genetics.md) - basic research and candidate gene analysis in racing pigeon genetics (LDHA, DRD4, CRY1 and others).
- [Performance research](performance.md) - genetic and physiological research on flight performance, including candidate gene association evidence.
- [Homing research](homing.md) - genetic and behavioural research on homing and navigation ability.
- [Endurance research](endurance.md) - physiological and genetic research on long-distance endurance performance.
- [Muscle physiology](muscle-physiology.md) - research on muscle development and exercise physiology.
- [Genetic markers](genetic-markers.md) - identification and application of genetic markers (SNP/microsatellite).
- [DNA testing](dna-testing.md) - application of DNA testing technology in racing pigeons.
- [Methodology](methodology.md) - quantitative genetics and molecular testing methods.

## Reading advice

- For conclusions about specific genes, check them against [evidence grading](evidence-grading.md) to judge their strength;
- Before citing a paper, verify the original through its DOI/PubMed link; this site provides traceable sources in the [literature index](../papers/index.md);
- All research conclusions are association evidence and do not constitute a prediction of an individual bird's performance.
