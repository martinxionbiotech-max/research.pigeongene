# Racing Pigeon Research Center

The PigeonGene Research Center (research.senopigeon.com) is the research backbone of the PigeonGene digital ecosystem, collecting **real and traceable** racing pigeon scientific literature and methodology.

## Our principles

- **Never fabricated**: every paper's DOI and PubMed record can be traced and verified
- **Scientifically rigorous**: association, correlation, causation and hypothesis are kept strictly apart
- **Honest wording**: gene associations are described as "suggests / may be associated", never exaggerated

- **Fanciers**: start from the plain-language summary in the [literature index](papers/index.md); every paper has one;
- **Breeders**: focus on [evidence grading](research/evidence-grading.md) and [how to read the literature](research/reading-literature.md), and understand that association is not causation;
- **Researchers**: use the DOI/PubMed links on each page to trace the original text.

## FAQ

**Are all the papers on this site real?**

Yes. Every paper listed here has been checked against its PMID/DOI and can be traced back to PubMed or the publisher page. Preprints are explicitly marked as not peer reviewed.

**What do the A/B/C/D evidence grades mean?**

A = supported consistently by multiple independent studies; B = pigeon-specific, peer reviewed and statistically significant; C = single or preliminary pigeon study; D = biologically plausible but with limited pigeon evidence. Compare a paper's conclusion against its grade.

**How should an ordinary fancier use this site?**

Start from the plain-language summary in the literature index - each paper has one. Then keep the core principle in mind: association is not causation, and treat conclusions as breeding reference only.
