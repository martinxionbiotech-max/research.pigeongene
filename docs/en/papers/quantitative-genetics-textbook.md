# Introduction to Quantitative Genetics

**Published title**: Introduction to Quantitative Genetics

**Authors**: Falconer DS and Mackay TFC (1996)

**Journal**: Textbook

## Plain-language summary (an analogy)

The textbook is like **a user manual for quantitative genetics** - it sets out core concepts such as heritability and breeding value in a systematic way. For pigeon breeding it is the theoretical basis for interpreting how a complex trait (performance) is inherited: a reference book for scientific breeding.

## Abstract

A foundational work in quantitative genetics that systematically explains genetic principles, heritability and methods for analysing quantitative traits, providing theoretical background for interpreting genetic markers in breeding.

## Research question

How can the genetic basis of quantitative traits be analysed scientifically?

## Background

Complex traits such as flight performance in racing pigeons are quantitative traits, and their genetic analysis requires dedicated theoretical methods.

## Methods

The textbook systematically introduces the theory and methods of quantitative genetics.

## Findings

It sets out core quantitative genetics concepts such as heritability, additive effects and breeding value.

## Interpretation of results

It provides a theoretical framework for interpreting genetic markers in pigeon breeding and counts as methodological groundwork.

## Limitations

As a textbook, it provides a theoretical framework rather than specific research data on racing pigeons.

## Significance

It provides methodological guidance for scientific breeding and the interpretation of genetic markers.

**Methodology (methodology)**
