# Pigeon Reference Genome and Crest Diversity

**Published title**: Genomic diversity and evolution of the head crest in the rock pigeon

**Authors**: Michael D. Shapiro et al. (2013)

**Journal**: Science

**DOI**: 10.1126/science.1230422

## Plain-language summary (an analogy)

The study is like **drawing the genome map of the rock pigeon** - it completed whole-genome sequencing of the domestic pigeon and became the standard base map for all later gene research. Without that map there is no way to pinpoint where a performance gene sits - it is the foundation project of racing pigeon genetics.

## Abstract

The study completed reference genome sequencing of the rock pigeon (Columba livia) and, through genomic analysis of the head crest trait, revealed a key gene controlling diversity in feather morphology. It is landmark work in pigeon genetics and provides the genomic basis for later research into performance genetics in racing pigeons.

## Research question

What genomic basis underlies the rich phenotypic diversity of the rock pigeon (head crest, plumage colour, body size)?

## Background

The rock pigeon is a classic model organism for studying morphological diversity and artificial selection. Darwin used domestic pigeons as an example of artificial selection in On the Origin of Species. Genomic resources for the pigeon were long lacking, however, which limited research into the genetic mechanisms.

## Methods

The team completed sequencing and assembly of the rock pigeon reference genome and, combining resequencing data from multiple breeds, carried out population genomic analysis and candidate gene mapping.

## Findings

The study revealed the key gene for the head crest trait and gave an initial overall picture of genomic diversity in the rock pigeon, providing an important genomic resource for later research.

## Interpretation of results

The study established the foundational framework for pigeon genome research. For racing pigeon research it provides a reference genome for comparison and stands as a foundational paper for understanding performance gene loci, genetic markers and the inheritance behind breeding.

## Evidence grade

**Genomic Resource (foundational resource research)** - it provides a reference genome and baseline data; it does not by itself directly demonstrate a performance association, but it supplies a necessary condition for downstream research.
